Rules of Engagement

Failure to follow these rules will result in becoming zombie chow (or a ban). Safety is our number one priority.

The Core Mechanic

  • Humans: Wear a bandana on your ARM. You can stun zombies with Nerf blasters or balled-up socks.
  • Zombies: Wear a bandana on your HEAD. You must tag humans with your hand to infect them.
  • Stunning: If a zombie is hit, they are stunned for 15 minutes. They must remove their headband or drop it around their neck.

Safe Zones

No gameplay is allowed inside ANY campus building. Academic buildings, dorms, and dining halls are strictly strictly off-limits for combat.

Wheelchair ramps and stairs leading to buildings are also safe zones.

Equipment

  • Nerf/foam dart blasters (painted distinctly, no realistic colors).
  • Clean socks rolled up and taped (sock bombs).
  • NO melee weapons, shields, or realistic-looking replicas.

*These rules are a summary. Full rulebook provided at orientation.
