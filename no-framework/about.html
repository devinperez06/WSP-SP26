About the Club

We are a student-run organization at SUNY New Paltz dedicated to organizing and running week-long games of Humans vs. Zombies.

Our Mission

To provide a fun, safe, and immersive experience for the student body, fostering teamwork, strategy, and getting some exercise while running for your life across the quad.

History

Founded years ago by survivors of the first outbreak, our club has grown to host hundreds of players every semester. What started as a small group of students with nerf blasters has evolved into a campus-wide phenomenon.
